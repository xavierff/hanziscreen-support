Hanzi Screen Support

Hanzi Screen helps users learn Mandarin vocabulary through flashcards, pronunciation practice, quizzes, tone practice, handwriting practice, favorites, and iPhone widgets.

Widget Help

To add the Hanzi Screen widget, open the iPhone widget gallery, search for Hanzi Screen, and add the widget to your Home Screen or Lock Screen.

You can choose which vocabulary appears in the widget from Settings inside Hanzi Screen.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Hanzi Screen is a Mandarin vocabulary learning app. An account is not required to study the free lessons.

Data handled by the app

Learning preferences and progress, including favorites, study history, personalized vocabulary, and widget settings, are stored on your device. If you use photo vocabulary import or speaking practice, the app requests permission to access your selected photos, camera, microphone, or speech recognition as necessary for those features.

Premium purchases

Subscriptions and lifetime purchases are processed by Apple using in-app purchases. Hanzi Screen uses RevenueCat to manage subscription status, purchase entitlements, and restore purchases. The purchase service may process purchase transaction identifiers, an app-specific user identifier, purchase history, and device or technical information needed to provide Premium access. We do not receive your complete payment-card details.

Audio and speech

Pronunciation playback and speaking practice may use iOS device speech capabilities and configured speech-related services. Microphone or speech recognition features are activated for user-initiated exercises and depend on your permissions. Review Apple's Privacy Policy for Apple service processing.

Promotional content

The app may display sponsored or promotional lessons. We do not claim here that every version is advertising-free. We do not sell your personal information to advertisers.

Sharing and retention

Local learning data remain on your device unless you elect to share or transfer them or use a feature that requires a service provider. You can delete local learning data using supported app and device controls. Transaction records and any service-provider records may be retained under Apple and RevenueCat policies and applicable law.

Your choices

You can decline or revoke camera, microphone, photo, and speech recognition permissions through iOS Settings, manage or cancel Apple subscriptions through your Apple account, and contact us regarding privacy questions.

This policy should be read together with any Apple-provided privacy notices. The App Store privacy-label disclosures must accurately reflect the current shipping app and its SDKs.
