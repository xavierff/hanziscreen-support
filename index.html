Hanzi Screen Support

Hanzi Screen helps users learn Mandarin vocabulary through flashcards, pronunciation practice, quizzes, tone practice, handwriting practice, favorites, and iPhone widgets.

Support

If you need help with Hanzi Screen, contact:

hanzionscreen@gmail.com

Widget Help

To add the Hanzi Screen widget, open the iPhone widget gallery, search for Hanzi Screen, and add the widget to your Home Screen or Lock Screen.

You can choose which vocabulary appears in the widget from Settings inside Hanzi Screen.

Privacy Policy

Hanzi Screen does not require users to create an account.

Hanzi Screen stores learning preferences and progress locally on the user's device, including favorites, study progress, theme preferences, and widget settings.

Hanzi Screen does not sell personal information.

Hanzi Screen does not currently use advertising or third-party analytics for tracking users.

If future versions introduce features that collect or process additional data, this privacy policy will be updated accordingly.
