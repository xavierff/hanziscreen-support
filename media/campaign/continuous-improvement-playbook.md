# Continuous content improvement — all owned publishing automations
Effective 2026-11-24 (Asia/Jakarta). Owner-authorized standing instructions. This playbook is not a new automation, permission grant, source of invented statistics, or override of publication schedules and safety holds.

## Low-cost daily learning cycle inside EXISTING scheduled Work runs
1. **Observe cheaply.** Use cached last 7–14 days of provider-confirmed performance and comments when already connected; one bounded provider analytics read at most if needed/available. Distinguish actual views, impressions, watch time, first 3s retention, completion, shares/sends/saves, follows/subscribers, genuine replies from guessed metrics. No separate Work run for research or monitoring.
2. **Find what worked.** Compare recent strong and weak posts from the SAME channel. Note hook, topic/phrase, story structure, visual framing, title/thumbnail, length, voice/pacing, audience reaction; small samples are directional only. Preserve a small durable "worked / didn't / next test" ledger without writing lengthy reports.
3. **Test ONE creative change at a time** in next unique source (hook, scene change, story resolution, phrase novelty, title, thumbnail, or pacing), preserve successful account identity. Compare next available 3–7 posts; never claim causal lift without evidence. Keep a balanced catalog and avoid repeating a Mandarin phrase, fictional device, or joke.
4. **Research sparingly.** During an existing preparation run AT MOST once per seven days per brand, one brief source lookup of contemporary platform creator guidance, real Mandarin colloquialisms (fact-check character/tones/translation), or a genuinely relevant emerging topic. Reuse verified own footage and insights first. Do not trend-chase unverified claims or reuse other creators' copyrighted material; if no browsing access/quota, use grounded evergreen ideas.
5. **QA before any publication.** A technically decodable MP4 is not necessarily correct. English, simplified Hanzi, tone-marked pinyin, meaningful translation, spoken Mandarin/English, caption/source manifest, spelling and font spacing must agree with story. Verify narration humanlike naturalness with owner-approved voice baseline, not merely waveform. No old hardcoded "It's okay" over unrelated Chinese lesson; use normal Latin English font/apostrophes; distinct source SHA and channel dedupe. On concrete fail, HOLD; don't consume weekly quota churning defective video or silently approve.
6. **Measure outcomes honestly.** A scheduled reservation is not public. Report provider IDs/links and next uncovered slot, add one short learning note. Escalate owner only for sign-in, license/content approval, unusual voice pronunciation, hard provider quota, or proven repeated failures. No purchases or extra recurring tasks without approval.
