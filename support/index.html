Quick help

Home Screen widget

Touch and hold an empty area on your Home Screen, tap Edit or the + button, search for Hanzi Screen, then choose a widget size and add it.

Lock Screen widget

Touch and hold your Lock Screen, choose Customize, select the Lock Screen, then tap a widget area and choose Hanzi Screen.

Widget vocabulary

Open Hanzi Screen and use Settings to choose which vocabulary source your widgets should display, such as all words, HSK levels, Everyday, or Favorites.

Audio & pronunciation

Make sure your iPhone volume is audible. Hanzi Screen uses the Mandarin voices available on your device and may sound slightly different depending on iOS.

Speaking practice

Speaking practice needs Microphone and Speech Recognition permission. You can review these permissions in iPhone Settings if recognition does not start.

Learning progress

Favorites, learning preferences, progress, themes, and widget settings are stored locally on your device.

Frequently asked questions

The widget is not appearing in the widget gallery.

Open Hanzi Screen once after installation, then return to the widget gallery. If it still does not appear, restart the iPhone and check again.

Why does the widget not change immediately?

iOS controls widget refresh timing to protect battery life. Hanzi Screen provides updated content to the widget, but the exact refresh moment is ultimately scheduled by iOS.

Can I choose which words appear in the widget?

Yes. Open Settings inside Hanzi Screen and select the vocabulary source you want the widget to use.

Does Hanzi Screen require an account?

No. Hanzi Screen does not require you to create an account to use the app.

How do I report a bug?

Email felix@hanziscreen.com with a short description, your iPhone model, iOS version, and a screenshot or screen recording if possible.
